Yes. A ticket agent may retain a service charge even if a passenger is entitled to a refund for a cancelled or significantly changed flight — but only if that charge is for ticket‑issuance/service (not merely for processing payment), is assessed on a per‑passenger basis, and was clearly and prominently disclosed to the consumer at the time of purchase (including the amount and, if applicable, that it is non‑refundable). The Department treats undisclosed fees as deceptive. Also, refunds must be provided in the original form of payment unless the consumer agrees otherwise [3][5].
