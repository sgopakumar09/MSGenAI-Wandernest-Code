Yes — but only in specific circumstances. Ticket agents may retain a service charge related to issuing the original ticket even if the passenger did not travel because the airline cancelled or significantly changed the flight, provided that:
- the fee is for services beyond merely processing payment for a flight the consumer found (i.e., not a pure booking/payment-processing fee) [3][5];  
- the fee is assessed on a per‑passenger basis [3][5]; and  
- the existence, amount, and (if applicable) the non‑refundable nature of the fee were clearly and prominently disclosed to the consumer at the time of purchase. Undisclosed fees would be treated as a deceptive practice [3][5].

Also note: refunds must be made in the original form of payment unless the consumer agrees otherwise [5].
