Yes. Ticket agents may retain a service charge for issuing the original ticket even if the airline cancels the flight and a refund is due, but only if:

- the fee is for ticket‑issuance or other services beyond merely processing payment (not a simple “booking/payment” fee) [3][5];  
- the fee is assessed on a per‑passenger basis; and  
- the existence, amount, and (if applicable) the non‑refundable nature of the fee were clearly and prominently disclosed to the consumer at the time of purchase [3][4][5].

The Department considers undisclosed fees to be deceptive and would not allow retention in that case [3]. (The Department did not resolve whether a pure booking/payment processing fee may be retained by a ticket agent) [2][1].
