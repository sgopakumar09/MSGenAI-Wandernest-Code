Air India defines "churning" as the process of repeated bookings for the same sector (or overlapping sectors) for the same passenger, made through a PNR or across multiple PNRs on the same GDS or across various GDSs by an agent [1]. Air India permits up to 4 churns in Economy (10 in Premium); thereafter an ADM of US$10 is charged for each additional churn per passenger per segment [1][2].
